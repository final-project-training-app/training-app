# PRD: [Project Name]

## Mandatory User Flow
1. **Landing:** Immediate understanding of product value.
2. **Onboarding:** Quick account creation.
3. **Planning:** Receive a workout recommendation or plan.
4. **Execution:** Complete a step-by-step guided activity.
5. **Feedback:** Receive a post-activity response or AI feedback.

## Feature Priority
* **Must Have:** User profiles, hardcoded workout library, guided exercise flow, completion logging, and basic progress tracking.
* **High Priority:** Personalized recommendations, conversational AI coach (voice/text), and session controls (pause/stop).
* **Medium/Low Priority:** Weekly schedules, reminders, and light/dark mode.

## UX Principles
* **Tone:** Kind, warm, and calm.
* **Navigation:** Clear buttons, minimal steps, and friendly language.
